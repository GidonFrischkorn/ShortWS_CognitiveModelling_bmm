# Reading list

Tutorial papers and background reading for each workshop segment. PDFs are
included in the segment subfolders **only where licensing permits** (open
access or preprint versions); everything else is listed with a DOI/URL only.

> Presenters: when you add a reading, list it here with its DOI and only drop
> the PDF into your subfolder if it is open access or a preprint you may
> redistribute. This repo is public.

**The PDFs in this folder are not covered by the repository license.** Each
paper remains copyright its authors and publisher, under whatever terms it
was published (CC-BY for the open-access ones). The workshop presenters claim
no rights over them, and the repository's non-commercial terms neither extend
to nor restrict them.

## General / bmm

- Frischkorn, G. T., & Popov, V. (2025). A tutorial for estimating Bayesian
  hierarchical mixture models for visual working memory tasks: Introducing
  the Bayesian Measurement Modeling (bmm) package for R. *Behavior Research
  Methods*, 57, 144. <https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-025-02643-0>
  — **PDF in `01_sdt/`** (open access, © The Authors)
- Popov, V., & Frischkorn, G. T. (2024). *bmm: Easy and Accessible Bayesian
  Measurement Models Using 'brms'* (R package).
  <https://cran.r-project.org/package=bmm> — link only
- Bürkner, P.-C. (2017). brms: An R package for Bayesian multilevel models
  using Stan. *Journal of Statistical Software*, 80(1).
  <https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v080.i01>
  — **PDF in `01_sdt/`** (open access)
- Etz, A., & Vandekerckhove, J. (2018). Introduction to Bayesian inference
  for psychology. *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review*, 25, 5–34.
  <https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-017-1262-3>
  — **PDF in `01_sdt/`** — ⚠️ redistribution terms unverified, check before
  the repo goes public
